Division 01 · Healthcare & Staffing

Qualified healthcare personnel and support for government health programs.

Government health programs depend on having the right people in place. Prestigia pursues healthcare staffing and support requirements and builds the team to fill them, with leadership from an experienced registered nurse.

Capability groups

What this division covers

Each engagement is scoped to the specific requirement and staffed with appropriately qualified personnel.

HC-01

Nursing staffing

Registered nurses, licensed practical and vocational nurses, and nursing support roles for clinics, facilities, and health programs.

  • Defined by setting, shift pattern, and licensure
  • Coverage for vacancies or added capacity
HC-02

Allied health and medical personnel

Technologists, therapists, medical assistants, and other allied roles, matched to the specialty and licensing requirements in the solicitation.

  • Role definitions written against the requirement
  • Licensure and credential needs identified up front
HC-03

Administrative and support staff

Patient access, medical records, scheduling, front office, and program administration personnel who keep clinical operations running.

  • Front office and records support
  • Program and administrative coordination
HC-04

Operational support

Coordination, onboarding tracking, and reporting that keep a staffing engagement aligned with the contract’s terms.

  • Onboarding and status tracking
  • Contract reporting and communication

When agencies engage us

Common healthcare requirements

  • Filling vacancies against a defined staffing requirement
  • Adding coverage for a new or expanding program
  • Supporting administrative workload in clinical settings
  • Standing up personnel for a time-limited effort

Clinical leadership

Our healthcare division is led by an experienced registered nurse (RN, BSN) who brings a clinician’s view to requirement review, role definitions, and partner communication.

Responsibility boundaries

Staffing and managed support are different offers.

Some requirements call for qualified personnel. Others call for ongoing coordination and management of the work. We define which one applies before the proposal is written.

Each engagement states who sources and employs the personnel, who verifies credentials, and who directs daily work. Clinical services are performed by appropriately licensed professionals under the terms of the engagement.

Additional clinical specialties and telehealth requirements are evaluated case by case.
