Flexible roles

We structure the team around the opportunity.

Prestigia as prime

You subcontract to us

We hold the prime contract and manage the customer relationship. Your firm performs its defined scope.

Prestigia as sub

We subcontract to you

Your firm primes. We contribute proposal development, compliance coordination, and our assigned share of the work.

Other team structures

Built to the requirement

Where a solicitation or set-aside calls for a different arrangement, we discuss it on a case-by-case basis.

Who we look for

Firms with capabilities that match real requirements.

  • Healthcare staffing firms and clinical service providers
  • IT support, cybersecurity, and software firms
  • AI, data, and automation specialists
  • Capable companies ready to pursue government work

What to expect

  1. 01

    Introductory conversation

    We learn what your firm delivers and where it has performed.

  2. 02

    Opportunity match

    When a requirement fits, we share it and confirm mutual interest.

  3. 03

    Written agreement

    A non-disclosure agreement and teaming agreement set roles and terms.

  4. 04

    Proposal collaboration

    We lead the proposal with your technical input and pricing.

Set up per opportunity

We do not promise work volume or exclusivity in advance. Each party’s responsibilities are written into the agreement for that opportunity.
