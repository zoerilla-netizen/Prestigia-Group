Division 02 · Technology

IT, cybersecurity, software, and AI support that fits how your program works.

Technology requirements succeed when the work fits the operation it supports. Prestigia pursues IT and technology requirements, brings in specialists for the technical scope, and coordinates delivery against the contract.

Service groups

Six areas, each tied to an operational need.

IT support and operations

Help desk and end-user support, infrastructure and network support, and IT asset management for day-to-day operations.

Cybersecurity support

Vulnerability management, security operations support, and compliance documentation such as Risk Management Framework artifacts.

Software and systems integration

Application development and modernization, system integration, data migration, and maintenance of existing systems.

AI and automation

Practical uses such as document processing, workflow automation, and data analysis, applied with human review and security requirements in mind.

Technical staffing

Qualified IT, cybersecurity, and software personnel placed against defined labor categories.

Health IT

Requirements where health and technology meet, evaluated with clinical and technical input from the start.

Practical applications

The kind of work this covers

Illustrative examples of requirements in scope for this division:

  • Automating intake of forms that staff currently re-key by hand
  • Preparing a system’s security documentation ahead of an authorization review
  • Providing tier 1 and tier 2 help desk coverage for an office or program
  • Migrating data from a legacy application to a supported platform

Specialized delivery

The right specialists for each technical scope.

For each requirement, we identify technical partners whose skills, certifications, and past work match the scope. Partners provide technical input and pricing during the proposal and perform their agreed work after award.

Prestigia leads the pursuit, coordinates the team, and manages its assigned contract responsibilities. Our Managing Director leads this division directly.

Where health and technology meet

Health IT requirements draw on both divisions, so we review them with clinical and technical input from the first read of the solicitation.
