About Prestigia

An accountable contracting company built around the right team for each requirement.

Prestigia Group LLC is a government contractor headquartered in Tampa, Florida. We focus on government healthcare and staffing, and on IT, cybersecurity, software, and AI requirements.

Purpose

Connect government requirements with the people and companies qualified to meet them.

Our approach is direct. We identify suitable opportunities, find qualified people and companies, build the right team, submit competitive proposals, and perform our agreed responsibilities after award.

We may prime, subcontract, or team depending on the opportunity. In every case, Prestigia’s contribution is defined: opportunity evaluation, proposal development, compliance coordination, team coordination, and assigned contract management.

We are building this company deliberately. Rather than claim a standing bench in every specialty, we assemble the qualified team each requirement calls for and put each party’s responsibilities in writing.
