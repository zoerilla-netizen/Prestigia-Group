How an engagement comes together

From solicitation to performance, with clear ownership at each stage.

  1. 01Pre-solicitation

    Evaluate the requirement

    We review the scope, evaluation criteria, and timeline, and decide whether we can field the right team before committing to pursue.

  2. 02Teaming

    Build the team

    We identify qualified personnel and companies for the specialized work and put each party’s role in a written agreement.

  3. 03Proposal

    Develop the proposal

    We lead writing and compliance coordination. The people who will perform the work supply the technical input and pricing.

  4. 04Post-award

    Perform and manage

    After award, we carry out our assigned contract management responsibilities and coordinate the team against the agreed scope.
