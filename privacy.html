Privacy Policy

How Prestigia Group LLC handles information submitted through prestigiagroup.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit through our forms or by email, such as name, agency or company, email address, phone number, and the content of your message.
  • Basic technical information logged by our hosting provider, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance.

We do not use advertising trackers on this site. Please do not submit patient information, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal data through our forms.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, evaluate requirements and potential teaming relationships, and operate and secure the site. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the site or handle submissions, with prospective team members when you have asked us to pursue an opportunity together, and where required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep information as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes and our business records. You may contact us to request correction or deletion, subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements.
